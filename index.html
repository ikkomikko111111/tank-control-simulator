Tank graphic shows simulated physical level. Sensor reads INVALID during an injected fault. The independent high-high switch trips at 90%.

Operator controls

AUTO: starts below 30%, stops at 80%, retains its state between limits. Faults stop the pump in both modes and latch a trip. Clear faults, lower level below 90%, then reset the trip. AUTO may restart after reset; MANUAL needs a new start command.

Educational browser simulation, not a PLC runtime or a validated industrial safety system. Model: pump adds 5 percentage points/second; open outlet removes 2 percentage points/second. No physical hardware connected.
